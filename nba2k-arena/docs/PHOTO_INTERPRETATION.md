# Photo Interpretation

All five reference photos are treated as views of one combined arena. People, clothing, vehicles and personal objects are excluded from the visual target.

## High confidence

- The venue is an outdoor oceanfront basketball complex under a clear blue sky.
- The court surface is medium neutral gray asphalt with low-gloss/matte response.
- Gameplay lines are white; there is no clearly visible center logo or school branding.
- Basket supports are dark gray/black metal with transparent backboards and orange rims.
- The surrounding hardscape is light gray concrete.
- One side includes silver aluminum bleachers.
- Other edges include stepped green lawns with concrete retaining faces.
- Tall palm trees are the dominant background identity.

## Medium confidence

- The asphalt has fine aggregate variation, mild wear and patching rather than a perfectly uniform coating.
- The grass is a restrained olive/coastal green rather than saturated turf.
- Major structural trim should be dark gray, while seating and rail components should read as galvanized/aluminum metal.
- Distant ocean and sky can be represented on donor background/mural surfaces to create the outdoor impression.

## Low confidence

- Exact text or small signage on the backboards and distant buildings is not readable enough to reconstruct safely.
- The full arrangement of palms outside every unseen side is unknown.
- The real venue has no enclosing roof, while the donor is enclosed. The safe first-pass compromise is a sky-colored ceiling/background treatment rather than deleting donor geometry.
- Exact scoreboard treatment is not visible. The donor score cube is therefore retained structurally and visually neutralized.

## Deferred visual upgrades

- A rectified palm/ocean panorama can replace the flat sky continuation after a compatible BC7/BC1 import path is proven in game.
- More detailed grass and concrete albedo tiles can be added without changing geometry.
- Small non-colliding palm silhouettes or background cards are optional only after an in-game camera test shows they are needed.
